Our Story & Roots

The Journey of KoshiWorkshop

From a humble village smithy repairing plowshares to establishing KOSHI Agrotech, our commitment to farmers has remained our driving force.

Phase 1: The Beginning

Traditional Blacksmithing & Agricultural Repair

Founded as an artisan workshop focused on forging traditional agricultural hand implements, sharpening plow blades, and servicing farm tools for local farming households in the Koshi region.

Phase 2: Modern Fabrication

Adopting Power Machinery & Structural Metalcraft

Upgraded our workshop floor with pneumatic power hammers, heavy lathe machines, hydraulic presses, and precision welding rigs to manufacture high-durability tractor implements and trailers.

Phase 3: The Present Era

Launching KOSHI Agrotech Manufacturing

Formalized our machinery brand KOSHI Agrotech. Today we supply chaff cutters, multi-crop threshers, and custom agro-machinery directly to farm cooperatives, tea estates, and agricultural development initiatives across Nepal.
